⌨️ 5. Admin Commands — Full Reference

You send these commands to the Bot Admin WhatsApp number (separate from the guest-facing bot). All commands start with /.

CommandWhat it does
/statusSee active guests, handover queue, AI status, paused users
/pause [phone]Manually pause AI for a specific guest (you take over)
/resume [phone]Resume AI auto-replies for a guest after you're done
/history [phone]Show recent conversation history with a guest
/listList all recent conversations + their status
/ai offTurn off ALL AI auto-replies (emergency brake)
/ai onTurn AI back on
/viewkbBot sends you the current Knowledge Base as a text message
/updatekbBot replies asking for new KB content → you paste → bot saves it
/helpShow all available commands
💡 Owner replies to guests directly on WhatsApp. When a guest triggers handover, the bot pauses. You just open your WhatsApp chat with that guest — the bot won't respond. You reply as the host. When done, send /resume [phone] to the admin bot and AI takes over again.

🔄 7. Handover Flow — Step by Step

  1. Guest asks something the AI can't answer from KB
  2. Bot replies: "I'm not sure — connecting you to the host" (in guest's language)
  3. Bot sets handed_over = true for that guest — AI stops responding
  4. You receive a Telegram/WhatsApp alert with guest name + question
  5. You reply directly on WhatsApp to the guest — your messages go through, AI stays silent
  6. When done, send /resume [phone] to the admin bot
  7. AI resumes — next guest message is handled by bot again
⚠️ You can also pause manually. If you see a guest conversation that needs a human touch, just /pause [phone] and jump in. No waiting for the bot to decide.
