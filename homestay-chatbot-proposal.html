1. Project Overview

What This Is

An AI-powered WhatsApp chatbot that handles guest inquiries 24/7 for Arau House in Perlis. It answers questions based on a Knowledge Base you control, automatically redirects booking requests to Agoda/Booking.com, and seamlessly hands over to you when a guest asks something the AI can't handle.

What It Does

💬

24/7 FAQ Auto-Reply

Guests ask about check-in, rates, parking, WiFi, house rules — the AI answers instantly from your Knowledge Base. Works in English and Bahasa Malaysia.

📅

Booking Link Redirect

When a guest wants to book, the bot sends direct links to Agoda and Booking.com. No payment or availability management needed.

🤝

Human Handover

If the AI can't answer, it politely connects the guest to you. The bot pauses for that guest — you take over manually on WhatsApp.

📱

Full Owner Control via WhatsApp

No web dashboard needed. You control everything by sending commands to your bot on WhatsApp. Pause, resume, check status, update Knowledge Base — all from your phone.

Key Benefits

  • Never miss a query — AI answers 24/7 even when you're asleep, at work, or on holiday
  • Reduce phone calls — 80-90% of guest questions are the same FAQs
  • Zero upfront cost — pay-as-you-go, ~RM 2-5/month total
  • No technical skills needed — you update the Knowledge Base by sending a WhatsApp message
  • Full control — handover to you instantly when needed, no confusing AI vs human overlap

2. System Architecture

How Messages Flow

  1. Guest sends a WhatsApp message to your WhatsApp Business number
  2. WhatsApp servers forward it to your cloud-hosted backend (webhook)
  3. Backend checks: Is this guest paused (handed over)? If yes → skip AI, forward to you
  4. If AI is active: Backend sends the question + Knowledge Base to AI (OpenAI/GPT-4o-mini)
  5. AI decides: Can answer from KB? → Reply to guest. Can't answer? → Trigger handover
  6. On handover: Backend marks guest as paused, notifies you, sends a handover message to guest
  7. You reply directly on WhatsApp — the bot will not respond to that guest
  8. When done: You send /resume [phone] to the admin bot → AI takes over again
💡 Key point: The bot never replies at the same time as you. When AI is paused for a guest, the backend simply ignores webhook messages from that phone number. Your manual WhatsApp replies go straight through.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need a separate phone number for the bot?

A: Yes, the WhatsApp Business API requires its own phone number. This can be a new SIM card or a virtual number. Your personal number stays as-is for personal use. However, the bot uses your existing WhatsApp — guests message one number, and both the AI and you can reply from it (just not at the same time).

Q: Can the bot send photos or documents?

A: Yes, the WhatsApp API supports images, PDFs, and location sharing. Want to auto-send a map or check-in guide? Easy to add.

Q: What happens if I'm asleep and the bot hands over?

A: The bot sends the handover message to the guest ("I'll connect you to the host") and pauses. The guest's message is saved. When you wake up, you'll see the handover notification and can reply. The bot doesn't block or ignore guests — it just stops auto-replying until you resume.

Q: Can I have the bot working during the day but off at night?

A: Yes. Send /ai off to the admin bot at night, /ai on in the morning. Or we can schedule it automatically.

Q: What if the AI gives a wrong answer?

A: The AI is strictly constrained to only answer from the Knowledge Base. It cannot make up rates, rules, or information. If the KB is correct, the AI's answers are correct. This is the key safety design.

Q: Do I need to know programming to update anything?

A: No. Updating the Knowledge Base = send a WhatsApp message. Turning AI on/off = send a WhatsApp message. Checking status = send a WhatsApp message. That's it.

Q: Can I reply to a guest without turning the AI back on?

A: Yes! When AI is paused (handover), the bot won't respond to that guest. You can have a full back-and-forth conversation. Only send /resume when you want the AI to take over again.

Q: What if a guest sends a voice message?

A: The bot can be configured to reply asking them to type instead. Voice-to-text is possible but adds cost. Handover on voice messages also works — you'll receive a notification and can reply manually.

Q: Is my guest data safe?

A: Yes. All conversation data is stored on the hosting platform. No third parties have access. WhatsApp messages are encrypted in transit. No SMS or email data is collected.

Q: Can I add more properties later?

A: Yes. The system can be extended to support multiple properties, each with its own Knowledge Base and WhatsApp number. This is an architecture decision we make early to keep options open.
