Owner Experience Summary

📱

Replying to Guests

You have two options:

  1. Web Portal — full chat UI on any browser
  2. WhatsApp directly — once AI pauses, you just reply on your own WhatsApp. The bot stops intercepting messages for that user.
🔌

No Extra Apps Needed

Every piece runs on your existing NUC:

  • ✅ Flask web app on NUC
  • ✅ Tailscale Funnel → public webhook
  • ✅ Telegram for urgent alerts
  • ✅ Browser for portal (any device)
🔄

Full Workflow

  1. Guest messages → AI handles 90%+
  2. Complex? → Telegram ping + AI pauses
  3. Open portal / WhatsApp → reply manually
  4. Done? → Click "Resume AI" → bot takes over again
